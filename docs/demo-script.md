## 0:50–1:15 — Any-component sourcing

“Here is a custom vibration isolator. We define the purchase requirements and compare the suppliers, but missing engineering and qualification evidence prevents a recommendation from becoming approval. A good score cannot override a failed mandatory check.”

## 1:15–1:40 — Real-company research

“Research searches public sources, extracts evidence and follows up on incident candidates. It distinguishes policy language from possible incidents, displays identity and freshness limitations, and links review actions to evidence.”

Only point to labels and actions actually present in this report. Do not claim an incident or follow-up happened if it did not.

## 1:40–2:00 — Defensible handoff
Download the decision packet.

“The buyer leaves with the brief, weighted screening, unresolved requirements and evidence references. It supports authorized review; it never automatically awards a contract.”

## Present limitations honestly
Chat currently uses deterministic supported routes. Nemotron reviews source excerpts; validated citations are not proof that its interpretations are correct. Company identity anchors and reviewer statements are not independently verified. Public deployment, jury login and the actual screen recording are still submission tasks.
